No Fog

A bendable nose wire is sewn invisibly into the top seam of the mouth panel, redirecting your breath down and away from your goggles. No visible clip. No hardware. Just a clean seam that happens to work.

02

Built to Breathe

One small, color-matched vent zone sits right where your breath actually escapes. Everywhere else stays solid, windproof fabric. Warmth where you need it, airflow where you don't.

Made for the Mountain

Fitted eye opening built for goggles. A full hood-to-neck cut that actually stays put. Designed by riders, for the runs that matter.

Built From a Dorm Room

PYKE started with one idea: riders shouldn't have to choose between gear that works and gear that looks right. We're not a big outdoor brand with a warehouse. We're a small team building real, manufactured gear from scratch — one product at a time — and documenting every step along the way.
